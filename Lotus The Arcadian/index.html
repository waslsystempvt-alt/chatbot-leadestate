Overview

Lotus Arcadian is an exclusive luxury residential development in Juhu, offering premium 3 & 4 BHK residences crafted with high-end finishes and thoughtfully planned amenities. Set in one of Mumbai’s most coveted neighbourhoods, the development combines refined urban living with easy access to Juhu Beach, key business districts, premium schools, healthcare and major transport corridors. About Lotus The Arcadian

Lotus Developers, now Sri Lotus Developers & Realty Limited, is a Mumbai-based real estate developer focused on luxury and ultra-luxury residential and premium commercial developments, with a strong presence in Mumbai’s western suburbs. The company specialises in redevelopment and joint development projects and manages the real estate value chain across design, construction, sales and customer engagement. With an established presence in Juhu and Andheri West, Lotus Developers focuses on premium locations, quality construction, thoughtful design and high-end living environments.
